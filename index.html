Workday

Seed collecting

Collect seeds from this year's plants. Take some home.

See how it works

Dried seed heads get rubbed over a sieve. The seeds fall through, the chaff stays behind. Then you spoon them into a small paper packet, write the name on it, and it goes home with you.

No experience needed. Sieves, trays, packets and markers are on the table.

13Sun
Workday

Compost bag giveaway prep

Bag up finished compost to give away. Gloves provided.

See what we do

The pile that has been cooking since spring gets turned, sifted and bagged. The bags go out free to whoever wants one. Shovels, sifters and bags are there. Wear shoes you do not mind getting dirty.

20Sun
Community event

Fall crop harvest

Pick this season's crops. Bring a bag to take some home.

See what a harvest day looks like

This is the month's public event. Whatever is ready in the beds gets picked, and you take some home.

Bring a bag. Everything else is provided, and there is nothing to sign up for.
